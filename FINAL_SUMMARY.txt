## WHAT'S IN YOUR NOTIFICATION

Each notification now includes:
- WHO did it (actor + actor_name + actor_profile_photo)
- WHAT did they do (notification_type: request|accept|reject|remove)
- WITH WHOM (related_user + related_user_name + related_user_profile_photo)
- WHAT ROLE (connection_role: expertise|skill_provider|supplier)

Different perspectives:
- Requester sees: "You requested to connect..."
- Receiver sees: "X requested to connect with you..."
